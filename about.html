About Us / Über Uns

Connecting People. Empowering Opportunities.

JobsPerHourBerlin.de is an innovative online platform based in Berlin, designed to make job searching and hiring fast, transparent, and local. We connect skilled individuals, freelancers, and part-time professionals with companies and private employers who need help — hour by hour, day by day.

Our mission is simple: to make employment in Berlin more flexible and accessible for everyone. Whether you're a business owner seeking short-term staff or a private user looking for reliable service providers, we’ve built a space where talent meets opportunity.

Community First

We believe in supporting the Berlin community by connecting local talent with real, nearby work opportunities.

Fair & Flexible Jobs

Our platform encourages fair work arrangements, giving job seekers and businesses freedom and flexibility.

Trust & Transparency

Every listing and applicant is handled with transparency and respect, ensuring safety and professionalism at all times.

Our Vision & Mission

We envision a Berlin where finding part-time work is as easy as booking a ride — where skills are valued, time is respected, and everyone can contribute meaningfully to their community.

Our mission is to simplify connections between job seekers and employers, reduce hiring time, and empower individuals to take control of their work-life balance through flexible hourly employment.
