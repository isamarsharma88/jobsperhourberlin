Learn more About Us

JobsPerHourBerlin connects students, international visitors, and local residents with flexible, hourly jobs across Berlin. Whether you’re seeking work or hiring help, we make the process simple, transparent, and multilingual — available in both English and German.

Our Mission

We aim to make finding and posting hourly jobs in Berlin easy, fast, and reliable. Our platform empowers students, international residents, and busy locals to find the right work opportunities or hire help for their daily tasks.

Why Choose JobsPerHourBerlin?

  • Wide variety of job categories, from household help to IT & design projects.
  • User-friendly platform available in English and German.
  • Trusted by students, freelancers, and local employers across Berlin.
  • Quick job posting and application process.
